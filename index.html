SignBridge: mapping spoken video to a sequence of Indian Sign Language concepts – read case study

An accessibility research prototype, documented stage by stage, including the experiments that failed.

  • Only 2 of its 209 signs are real sign clips; the other 207 are placeholder cards
  • On the sign HELLO, a reach-correction fix cut the avatar’s wrist-path error by about 85% versus the earlier approach; a later fix for arm clipping gave back part of that gain
  • On FRIEND, a sign where the hands touch, hand tracking found the right hand in only 34% of frames (local, uncommitted research)
Status
Research prototype, run locally; not deployed

Why product

Why I’m exploring product management roles

I started by building software, and over time I became more interested in the problems behind it. At PPFAS, business teams depended on a separate tech team to manage their workflows. I built static pages and management portals so they could manage those workflows themselves.

That is the part I want to do more of: working out why something should exist, who needs it and how the workflow should run. It is why I’m moving towards product management.
